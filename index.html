01 / ABOUT THE BOOK

Every change
begins with a thought.

The Secret of Mindset is a personal book project about understanding our thoughts, building self-awareness, and looking at everyday life with a curious and open mind.

Through simple ideas and reflections, this book aims to encourage readers to pause, ask questions, and discover how their way of thinking shapes their experiences.
